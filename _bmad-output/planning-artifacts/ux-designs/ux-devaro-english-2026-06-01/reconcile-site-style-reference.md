# Reconciliation - Supplied Style Reference

Input: `imports/site-template-style-reference.jpg`

## Lifted Into The Spines

- Torn-paper layers used selectively at backgrounds or section boundaries.
- Photocopy-halftone decorative sticker family.
- Dark grid fragments used as occasional collage layers.
- Lighter green structural color with pink accent layers.
- Bold condensed uppercase headings paired with readable sans-serif body copy.
- Compact printed text labels and square-edged blocks.

## Intentionally Dropped

- The banner's words, composition, recruitment purpose, and element placement.
- Dense poster treatment across every site section.
- Large padded text badges.
- Replacement or filtering of existing teacher photos.

The reference is inspiration only. `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` win on conflict.
